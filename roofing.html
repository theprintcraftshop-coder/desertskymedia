Overview

Clear aerial views of the roof without the ladder.

Our drone roof inspection service gives you a clear view of the roof from multiple angles using high-resolution aerial photos and video. Whether you are a roofing company documenting a job, a homeowner checking for possible damage, or a property manager reviewing a building, drone photography makes the inspection process faster, safer, and easier to understand.

This is useful for checking visible storm damage, missing shingles or tiles, roof wear, gutters, vents, flashing, flat roofs, commercial roofs, and hard-to-see areas.

FAQ

Common roofing questions.

Clear expectations help keep the service useful and professional.

Do you physically inspect the roof?

No. This service provides aerial photo and video documentation. It does not replace a licensed roof inspection, roofing contractor inspection, or insurance adjuster report.

Can drone photos show roof damage?

Drone photos can help document visible roof issues such as missing shingles, damaged tiles, debris, gutters, flashing, vents, and other visible concerns. Some problems may still require an in-person inspection.

How fast are photos delivered?

Standard delivery is usually digital delivery after the shoot. Rush and same-day delivery may be available for an added fee.

Can you work with roofing companies?

Yes. We offer single-job pricing and recurring partner pricing for roofing companies that need regular roof documentation.

Can you fly anywhere?

Flights depend on weather, location, airspace, property access, and FAA safety requirements.
